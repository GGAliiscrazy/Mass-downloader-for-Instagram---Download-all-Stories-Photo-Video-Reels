# Mass Downloader for Instagram

Download posts, Reels, Stories, and Highlights from Instagram profiles and other supported pages. Save everything available or choose specific content using date ranges, sequence numbers, filters, or manual selection.

## Download Posts and Reels

Save content from profiles, feeds, tagged posts, locations, and saved collections:

- Download all posts or Reels from a profile
- Filter posts and Reels by date range or sequence
- Select individual posts or Reels to download
- Download tagged posts and posts from locations
- Save posts from your collections, with optional date and sequence filters
- Filter Reels by audio and download matching results
- Save Reel cover images along with the Reels

## Download Stories and Highlights

Download Stories and Highlights directly from the Instagram website:

- Download all available Stories from a specific account
- Download Stories from accounts you follow
- Download a user’s Highlights

## Manage Large Downloads

Tools to help organize and handle larger archives:

- **Adaptive downloading:** Adjusts the download process to Instagram’s behavior to help reduce unnecessary browser load.
- **Automatic ZIP splitting:** Divides large downloads into multiple archives to make them easier to manage.
- **Custom naming:** Choose a ready-made filename pattern or create your own for files and archives.
- **No download limits:** Download without a paid subscription or additional payment for access to your downloaded files.
- **Collapsible menu:** Minimize the extension menu when it’s not in use.

Download an entire profile archive or select just the content you need.
